ALPHA & OMEGA SEMICONDUCTOR Ltd SEC 10-Q Report

閱讀2分鐘
AOSL

Alpha & Omega Semiconductor Ltd, a leading provider of power semiconductors, has released its Form 10-Q report for the second quarter of fiscal year 2026. The report highlights the company's financial performance and operational activities, reflecting the challenges and strategic initiatives undertaken during the period.

Financial Highlights

  • Total Revenue: $162.3 million, a decrease of 6.3% compared to the same quarter last year, primarily due to a decrease in sales of power discrete products and license and development services.
  • Gross Profit: $34.8 million, representing 21.5% of total revenue, down from 23.1% in the same quarter last year, reflecting a decrease in sales and changes in product mix.
  • Operating Loss: $(13.6) million, compared to an operating loss of $(5.9) million in the same quarter last year, driven by increased operating expenses.
  • Net Loss: $(13.3) million, compared to a net loss of $(6.6) million in the same quarter last year, impacted by lower revenue and higher operating expenses.
  • Net Loss Per Common Share: $(0.45), compared to $(0.23) in the same quarter last year, reflecting the increased net loss.

Business Highlights

  • Revenue Segments: The company reported a decrease in sales of power discrete products by 10.6% for the three months ended December 31, 2025, compared to the same period in 2024. However, sales of power IC products increased by 9.5% during the same period.
  • Geographical Performance: Revenue from Hong Kong increased to $155.8 million for the three months ended December 31, 2025, compared to $150.6 million in the same period in 2024. In contrast, revenue from China decreased significantly to $2.7 million from $15.9 million.
  • Sales Units: The net decrease in power discrete and power IC product sales was primarily due to a 5.7% decrease in unit shipments, partially offset by a 1.7% increase in average selling price due to a shift in product mix.
  • New Product Launches: During the six months ended December 31, 2025, the company introduced 37 new products, continuing its product diversification program by developing new silicon and packaging platforms.
  • New Production Launches: The company operates an 8-inch wafer fabrication facility in Hillsboro, Oregon, which is critical for accelerating proprietary technology development and new product introduction.
  • Future Outlook: The company expects to receive the remaining installment payment of approximately $15.8 million from the sale of its equity interest in the JV Company, which will provide additional capital for investment in technology and R&D projects.
  • Operational Strategy: The company is focused on diversifying its product portfolio and penetrating other market segments, including consumer, communications, and industrial markets, to reduce reliance on the declining PC market.

SEC Filing: ALPHA & OMEGA SEMICONDUCTOR Ltd [ AOSL ] - 10-Q - Feb. 05, 2026
